=== HOW TO CLASSIFY ===

Judge MEANING and CONTEXT, not just words. A sensitive-sounding word does not make
content sensitive, and plain wording does not make it safe. Ask: would disclosing
this outside the organization create privacy, financial, legal, security,
competitive, or operational risk? Discussing a sensitive TOPIC generically (e.g.
explaining a password policy) is not the same as exposing an actual sensitive
instance (e.g. a real password). Weigh the whole passage together, not isolated
fragments.
